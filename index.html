Deadlock mod merger

ShowRank+ and Top Bar Plus

Both mods replace the same two top bar layouts, so with both installed one of them loses its top bar. Merge ShowRank v1.0.0 and Top Bar Plus v5 into one VPK that keeps both. Your files stay in your browser.

  • Top bar ranks
  • Escape menu
  • Profiles
  • Rank history
  • Rejuv timer
  • Bridge timer
  • Recent purchases

Merge in three steps

  1. 01

    Top Bar Plus v5

    Download v5_top_bar_plus.zip from GameBanana and add it unchanged.

    or drop it here

  2. 02

    ShowRank v1.0.0

    Add one of the two downloads. Your result matches it.

    • showrank_barebones_v100.7zNormal, with MISSING tags and the ENEMY MISSING alert.
    • showrank_barebones_no_missing_v100.7zNo Missing, without those alerts.
    or drop it here

  3. 03

    Build and install

    Add both files to build.

    Remove the separate ShowRank and Top Bar Plus VPKs from Deadlock\game\citadel\addons, then put the new file there with a free pak name, for example pak89_dir.vpk.
